Master Elodin: The Complete Record

Curated canon facts, confirmed theories, and unresolved questions about Elodin from The Name of the Wind and The Wise Man's Fear. Use the filters to explore by category, evidence type, or confidence level.

How to Read This

Canon = direct book quotes. Theory = supported speculation. High/Med/Low = textual support confidence.

The Surface

A Master Namer at the University who teaches through chaos and riddles. Younger than his peers, unnervingly perceptive, and seemingly amused by things that horrify others.

The Depth

A former Chancellor who went too far in his studies, was confined to the Rookery, and escaped through pure Naming. He knows things he cannot—or will not—speak aloud.

The Mystery

His true agenda remains unknown. He protects certain people. He fears specific knowledge. He may be playing a longer game than anyone at the University realizes.
