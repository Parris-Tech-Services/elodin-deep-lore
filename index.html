Why Elodin Matters

Master Elodin is one of the most carefully constructed characters in the Kingkiller Chronicle. He is simultaneously more intelligent than almost everyone he meets, deliberately concealing knowledge, teaching through apparent chaos, and operating on a level of Naming that threatens the reality of the world itself.

This compendium serves two purposes: (1) to preserve every shred of textual evidence about who he is and what he knows, and (2) to support game designers, fan theorists, and writers who need to understand how to handle a character who feels unserious on the surface and dangerous underneath.
